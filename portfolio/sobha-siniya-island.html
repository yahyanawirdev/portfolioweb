A resort composition built around the dialogue between architecture, shoreline, and horizon. The aerial frame establishes spatial hierarchy, while the sunset view shifts toward an immersive, human-scale moment. Warm low-angle light grazes façades and water, creating layered reflections and tonal gradients. Travertine, glass, timber, and greenery carry restrained material variation. Atmospheric haze, controlled highlights, cinematic grading, and selective contrast unify the imagery into a refined photographic finish.
