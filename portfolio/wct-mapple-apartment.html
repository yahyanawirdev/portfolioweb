A refined interior visualization shaped around a restrained, editorial palette and a strong sense of spatial calm. Soft daylight is layered with warm architectural accents, creating gentle gradients and controlled contrast across the living and kitchen zones. Veined marble, satin cabinetry, brushed metal and plush upholstery are resolved with subtle roughness variation for tactile realism. Balanced framing establishes a clear visual hierarchy, while restrained grading and clean post-production preserve a natural, premium photographic finish.
