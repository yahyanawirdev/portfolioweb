A contemporary retail interior shaped around openness, rhythm, and a sense of everyday movement. Soft skylight and linear architectural illumination create an even luminous field, with subtle warm accents adding depth around the cafés and seating zones. Polished stone, glass, metal, and refined plaster surfaces provide controlled reflectance and tonal variation. The wide framing establishes strong spatial hierarchy, while figures and the sculptural centerpiece animate the scene. Delicate color grading, exposure balancing, and restrained contrast complete a clean editorial finish.
